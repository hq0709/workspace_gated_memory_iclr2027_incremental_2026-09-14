\section{Implications}
\label{sec:implications}

\paragraph{For builders of agent memory systems.}
Treat admission as a trainable decision in the memory pipeline.
A host can attach SDA to admission requests while keeping its reader
frozen. Qwen3-8B serves the standard pool at 242 candidates/s, making the learned
selection available through standard serving (\S\ref{sec:deployment}).

\paragraph{For builders of models.}
Train the importance interface to use rankings available in the model's
own computation. SDA improves development admission AUC at every tested
Qwen3 training scale and seed while workspace AUC changes by at most
0.0046 (App.~\ref{app:sdascale}), making prospective memory selection
a target for self-distillation.

\paragraph{For practitioners evaluating memory.}
Compare admission policies on the same episodes, with the reader and
budget fixed, useful-concept provenance balanced, and unstated
distractors present. Report within-episode ranking, retention split by
provenance, and frozen-reader recall to connect candidate scores to
stored information and answers.
