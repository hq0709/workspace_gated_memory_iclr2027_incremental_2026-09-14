\section{Implications}
\label{sec:implications}

Memory admission is a trainable component of an agent.
Self-Distilled Admission (SDA) trains the importance interface using
rankings from the model's own computation. A host can attach the
adapter only to admission requests and keep the reader frozen.
Qwen3-8B serves the standard pool through a standard serving engine
(\S\ref{sec:deployment}). SDA improves development admission AUC
at every tested Qwen3 training scale and seed. The workspace readout
AUC changes by at most 0.0046 (App.~\ref{app:sdascale}).

Admission evaluation should separate usefulness from provenance.
Compare policies on the same episodes with a fixed reader and budget.
Balance the provenance of the useful concept and include unstated
distractors. Report within-episode ranking, retention split by
provenance, and frozen-reader recall. Together, these measurements
connect candidate scores to the information stored and the answers
the reader produces.
