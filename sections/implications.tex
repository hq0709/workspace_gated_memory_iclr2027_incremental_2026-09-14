\section{Implications}
\label{sec:implications}

Admission is a trainable decision in the memory pipeline: a host can
attach SDA to admission requests while keeping its reader frozen.
Qwen3-8B serves the standard pool at 242 candidates/s, making learned
selection available through standard serving (\S\ref{sec:deployment}).
Training the importance interface to use rankings available in the
model's own computation makes prospective memory selection a target for
self-distillation. SDA improves development admission AUC at every tested
Qwen3 training scale and seed while workspace AUC changes by at most
0.0046 (App.~\ref{app:sdascale}).

Compare admission policies on the same episodes, with the reader and
budget fixed, useful-concept provenance balanced, and unstated
distractors present. Report within-episode ranking, retention split by
provenance, and frozen-reader recall to connect candidate scores to
stored information and answers.
