\section{Implications}
\label{sec:implications}

SDA raises pooled AUC on the development sets at every tested
Qwen3 training scale and seed, changing the admission score while
the adapter-enabled model's workspace readout AUC changes by at most
0.0046 (App.~\ref{app:sdascale}).

Separating usefulness from statedness in admission evaluation
requires comparing policies on the same episodes with a fixed
reader and budget, balancing the statedness of the useful concept,
and including unstated distractors. Evaluation should report
within-episode ranking, retention split by statedness, and answer
accuracy (\S\ref{sec:setup}).
