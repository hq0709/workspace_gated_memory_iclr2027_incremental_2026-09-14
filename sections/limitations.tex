\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The evaluation covers admission of host-supplied candidates in generated
episodes with one later question; end-to-end long-conversation memory
also requires candidate extraction, update, retrieval, and deletion.
Gold utility labels mark the generator's useful concept, while the
optimal memory can differ by reader. Stem matching approximates
statedness, and semantic grading uses an LLM judge. Serving and
full-candidate workspace readout measurements (which score every token
of a candidate) cover Qwen3-8B on the standard pool; serving uses batched
scoring on one A100-80GB. The objective ablation measures ranking and
answer accuracy, with three seeds at learning rate $10^{-5}$ and one at
each smaller learning rate.
Because the 54 model--seed--budget comparisons share episodes, they
measure consistency across settings, not 54 independent replications.
