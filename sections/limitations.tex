\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The authored episodes evaluate host-supplied candidate admission for
one later question; end-to-end long-conversation memory also requires
proposal, revision, retrieval and deletion. Construction labels identify
an intended bridge, not the optimal memory for every reader. Stem
matching approximates provenance, and semantic grading uses a model
judge. Serving measurements cover batched Qwen3-8B scoring on the
standard pool on one A100-80GB. Full-candidate readout measurements cover Qwen3-8B on the
standard pool. The objective ablation evaluates ranking and recall
rather than probability calibration, with three seeds at learning rate $10^{-5}$
and one at each smaller learning rate. The 54 model--seed--budget comparisons share episodes and measure
consistency across settings, not 54 independent replications.
