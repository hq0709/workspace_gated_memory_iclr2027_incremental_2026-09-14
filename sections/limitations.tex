\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The evaluation covers admission of host-supplied candidates in authored
episodes with one later question. End-to-end long-conversation memory
also requires proposal, revision, retrieval, and deletion. Construction
labels identify an intended bridge rather than the optimal memory for
every reader. Stem matching approximates provenance, and semantic
grading uses a model judge. Serving and full-candidate readout
measurements cover Qwen3-8B on the standard pool. Serving uses batched
scoring on one A100-80GB. The objective ablation measures ranking and
recall rather than probability calibration, with three seeds at learning
rate $10^{-5}$ and one at each smaller learning rate. The 54
model--seed--budget comparisons share episodes. They measure consistency
across settings rather than 54 independent replications.
