\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The importance report orders memory candidates by provenance,
favoring stated content even when the useful concept is unstated.
Self-Distilled Admission (SDA) uses a frozen model's workspace readout
and output likelihood to train a LoRA adapter through the model's own
importance prompt. Training requires no future questions, rewards, or
external teacher. SDA improves recall over each model's importance
report on the statedness-balanced pool. The adapter attaches only to
admission requests and preserves recall under standard serving.
This enables agents to decide what to retain before a question
arrives and to store inferred links that later answers need.
